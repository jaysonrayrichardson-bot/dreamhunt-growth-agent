Safeguards & Approvals

Built for a nonprofit telling stories involving children and sponsor relationships.

POLICIES ACTIVE
Family permission gate

No draft can move to “approved” when required image, video, name or story permissions are missing.

Human publishing approval

The agent drafts and schedules. A designated Dream Hunt administrator makes the final public decision.

Sponsor disclosure check

Sponsored or gifted relationships are flagged so platform-appropriate disclosure can be included.

Hunting-content review

Harvest, firearm and sensitive imagery are routed to review before monetization or broad distribution.

Medical-detail protection

Health or diagnosis details are excluded unless the family specifically authorizes them for the story.

Deliverable accountability

Every sponsor promise becomes a trackable obligation with due dates, evidence and completion status.
